[PRIMARY ROLE]

A hop counter decremented by 1 at every L3 router hop. When TTL reaches 0, the packet is discarded.

[ROUTING FUNCTION]

Primary routing loop killer. During dynamic routing convergence (e.g. OSPF/BGP flapping), packets caught in loops are dropped once TTL reaches 0, returning an ICMP Time Exceeded (Type 11) to the sender.

[SECURITY & NETWORK DEFENSE]

Critical defense against endless routing loops and packet storm meltdown. Also serves as the foundation for the traceroute utility and enables OS fingerprinting detection. Protects against BGP route poisoning storms.
